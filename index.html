    About Osh

    One of the oldest cities

    Osh is among the oldest cities in Central Asia, with a history of roughly 3,000 years on the Silk Road.

    UNESCO World Heritage

    Sulaiman-Too, the sacred mountain rising from the city, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

    Fergana Valley

    The city sits at the eastern edge of the fertile Fergana Valley, close to the Uzbek border.

    Cultural centre

    Often called the southern capital, Osh is a key cultural and historical hub of Kyrgyzstan.
